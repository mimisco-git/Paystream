01
Circle developer-controlled wallets

Every employer float and worker wallet is a Circle developer-controlled SCA wallet on Arc. No raw private keys, fully managed key infrastructure.

02
Arc Nanopayments for streaming

Nanopayments enable high-frequency sub-cent USDC transfers — ideal for per-minute salary disbursement at near-zero cost.

03
Circle Gateway for unified balance

Workers hold one USDC balance accessible on any supported chain. Circle Gateway provides instant minting to the destination chain in under 500ms.

04
CCTP + Bridge Kit for withdrawals

Workers pick their preferred chain at withdrawal time. CCTP routes the transfer with Fast Transfer completing in 8 to 20 seconds, any time of day.
